Study tools

Turn any study-plan topic into a full course — in one click

3 min read · Published 2026-10-22 · Updated 2026-10-22

Knowing you need to study "quadratic equations" on Tuesday is not the same as having something to actually learn from. StudYear closes that gap: next to every topic in your study plan is a 🎓 Course button, and one click turns that single line into a full, structured course built for your subject and level.

The USP: a plan line becomes a real course, instantly

No searching, no setup. Tap Course on any planned session and StudYear builds a proper course on that topic — to a high standard, pitched to your level, in seconds. Each module is written to teach, not to pad:

  • a clear learning objective,
  • a worked example that shows the method step by step,
  • the common mistake to avoid on that point,
  • and a check quiz at the end of the module so you prove you have got it before moving on.

Why one click changes everything

The hardest part of revision is starting. When the course is one tap from the plan you already have, the friction disappears — you go from "I should revise this" to "I'm learning it" without a detour. And because the course is generated for your exact subject and level, it is specific and relevant, not a generic article.

It fits the rest of your plan

The same topics flow through your plan, your interactive lessons and your resource search, so a course is never an island — it is one more way into the exact thing you are scheduled to study, with the check quizzes feeding your progress.

Is the course tailored to my level?

Yes. It is built from the subject, topic and level attached to that plan session, so a GCSE course and a primary course on the "same" subject look very different — each pitched where it should be.

Do I have to set anything up?

No. The one‑click course reads the topic straight from your plan. You tap once and start learning.
