How schools can evidence the Gatsby Benchmarks automatically

4 min read · Published 2026-08-02 · Updated 2026-08-03

Careers provision is no longer a nice-to-have — it is statutory, and with the 14+ technical-education reforms it is about to carry more weight in inspection. The framework schools are measured against is the Gatsby Benchmarks.

What the benchmarks ask for

The eight benchmarks cover a stable careers programme, good labour-market information, personal guidance, and — the hardest to evidence — real encounters with employers and experiences of workplaces. The Careers & Enterprise Company sets out the detail.

Why evidence is the bottleneck

Most schools deliver plenty of activity but struggle to prove it at inspection. Encounters happen; the audit trail does not always follow.

FAQ

Are the Gatsby Benchmarks statutory?

Every English secondary school must have regard to the eight Gatsby Benchmarks and is measured against them, so in practice they function as a statutory standard for careers provision.

Which Gatsby Benchmark is hardest to evidence?

Benchmarks 5 and 6 — encounters with employers and experiences of workplaces — are usually the hardest, because the activity happens but the evidence trail is often incomplete.
