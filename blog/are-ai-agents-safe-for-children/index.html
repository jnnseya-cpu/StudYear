Are AI agents safe for children? A parent's guide

3 min read · Published 2026-09-10 · Updated 2026-09-10

AI agents can do a lot for a child's learning. They can also do a lot with a child's data. Whether they are safe comes down to how they are built — and there are five questions any parent can ask to tell the difference.

Can AI agents be safe for children?

Yes, when they are designed for it. An agent that plans and acts on a child's behalf needs stronger guardrails than a simple chatbot, because it touches more data and does more. The UK's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code) sets the standard AI used by children should meet, and the government's guidance on generative AI in education is clear that safety and data protection come first.

The five questions every parent should ask

  • Does a human stay in control, or can the agent act unchecked?
  • What happens to my child's data — is it encrypted, and who can see it?
  • Is the AI ever allowed to train on my child's information?
  • Is it age-appropriate by design, and does it meet UK GDPR and KCSIE expectations?
  • Can my child be tracked or advertised to inside the learning area?

Green flags in a safe AI agent

When you are comparing tools, a few signs separate the safe from the careless. Look for a clear privacy policy written for families, not lawyers. Look for a named data-protection contact and a UK registration. Look for encryption described in plain terms, a stated position that the AI never trains on children's data, and controls that let a parent or school see and delete what is held. An agent that hides these, or buries them, is telling you something. And remember that free is not always the same as safe — check who pays, and how, before you decide. FAQ

Are AI agents safe for young children to use?

They can be, if they are age-appropriate by design, keep a human in control, encrypt data, and never train AI on a child's information. Check for UK GDPR, KCSIE and AADC compliance before trusting one.

Will an AI agent do my child's homework for them?

A well-designed education agent should teach the method, not hand over answers — the approach behind an AI tutor that teaches rather than cheats. That builds understanding instead of dependence.

What data does an AI study agent collect?

It varies by product. A safe one collects only what it needs to help, encrypts it, keeps it permissioned, and never sells it or trains external AI on it. Always check the privacy policy before you sign up.
