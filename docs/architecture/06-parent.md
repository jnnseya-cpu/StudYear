# 06 — Persona Teardown: Parent

> **The Governance & Support Layer.** A high-level **observability window** giving families
> real-time visibility into academic progress, behavioral indicators, and administrative
> balances — **without creating friction for the student.** The parent sponsors and
> supervises the learner; they act *on behalf of* their dependents through the guardianship
> relationship.

## 1. Role summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tenant scope** | Platform tenant (household); linked into school/tutoring tenants via their child |
| **Primary jobs** | Is my child on track? → what should I do? → do it (pay, book, approve, message) |
| **Reads** | child's grades, submissions, attendance, feedback, ledgers, alerts |
| **Writes** | payments, bookings, approvals/consents, messages |
| **Kernel services** | Identity & Tenancy (guardianship), Analytics, Commerce, Booking, Communication |

## 2. Core features & functionalities

### 2.1 Multi-Child Executive View Dashboard
A single portal letting parents with **multiple children — enrolled across different
classes or tutor programs — seamlessly toggle between accounts.**

*Architecture:* the household holds N guardianship edges. The dashboard is a
**household-scoped read-model** with a per-child context switcher; each child view composes
that child's Learning Records, Scheduling, and Commerce. One login, many dependents, strict
per-child data scoping.

### 2.2 Real-Time Performance Tracking
Live portals into **gradebooks, assignment submission histories, missing assignments, and
verified classroom attendance records.**

*Architecture:* read-only projections of the *same* records the teacher writes — no
parallel data. "Missing assignment" is a derived state (assignment exists ∧ past due ∧ no
submission), computed once in Analytics and surfaced identically to parent, student, and
teacher.

### 2.3 Automated Action Alerts & Flags
Configurable **email and push notifications**, triggered instantly when a child:
- misses a submission deadline,
- fails a quiz, or
- receives a disciplinary note.

*Architecture:* Analytics emits domain events (`deadline_missed`, `quiz_failed`,
`conduct_note_added`); the Notification service fans them out per the parent's channel/rule
preferences. These alerts are the **left edge of the closed loop** — the moment a parent is
prompted to seek help.

### 2.4 Integrated Tuition & Fee Ledger
A transparent financial dashboard for **paying school tuition, settling extracurricular
dues, and completing one-click invoice balances for private tutors.**

*Architecture:* Commerce aggregates obligations from two sources — the school tenant
(tuition/fees) and tutors (session/package invoices) — into one household ledger.
Balances, receipts, and payment methods are unified even though payouts route to different
recipients (institution vs. individual tutor).

### 2.5 Direct Educator Communication Line
A secure channel for **text updates and booking virtual parent-teacher conferences.**

*Architecture:* Communication threads are scoped to `(parent, child, educator)` so context
is preserved; conference booking reuses the same Scheduling/Booking service tutors use,
against the teacher's published office-hours availability.

## 3. Actions & micro-workflows

| Action | Flow | Services touched |
|---|---|---|
| **Review tutor feedback** — read a detailed lesson-by-lesson feedback summary generated by a private tutor immediately after a paid session | session completes → tutor posts structured feedback → parent notified & views | Booking, Communication, Analytics |
| **Authorize consent** — sign digital consent forms / permission slips for school excursions | form issued by school → parent e-signs → status returns to school + audit trail | Identity & Tenancy (consent), Communication |
| **Fund with conditions** — set up recurring payments or authorize **milestone funds released on successful completion of a tutoring package** | escrow/authorization held → release triggered by verified package completion | Commerce (escrow/authorizations), Booking |

## 4. Guardianship: the mechanism that makes this work

The parent surface is powered by the **guardianship edge** (Parent → Student):

- **Controller vs. observer.** Below a configurable age/policy threshold the parent is the
  account controller (approves bookings, spend, comms); above it the student self-serves
  with parental *visibility*. Tenant Admin sets the threshold and defaults.
- **Scoped delegation.** A parent's read access is *derived from* their children's records,
  never broader. Toggling children re-scopes every panel.
- **The closed-loop actor.** Alerts (2.3) → search & book a tutor (Booking + Marketplace)
  → pay (Commerce) → outcomes flow back into the *same* records the parent monitors. The
  parent is the persona that most often *closes* the loop described in `00-overview.md`.

## 5. Connection to the rest of the ecosystem

- Reads the **teacher's** gradebook and the **tutor's** feedback through one lens.
- Pays both the **institution** (school tenant) and **individual tutors** through one ledger.
- Is governed by **Tenant Admin** policy (consent thresholds, comms rules, fee structures).
- Acts for the **student** without adding friction to the student's own workspace.
