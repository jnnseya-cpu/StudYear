# StudYear — What It Is & Why People Buy It

**One-line positioning:** StudYear is the UK's AI learning operating system — one
platform that carries a child from EYFS to university and into their career, and
gives parents, teachers, schools and tutors the same live picture of how that
child is really doing.

---

## For Students & Learners

**AI Tutor that teaches, not cheats.** A personal tutor available 24/7 that walks
students through the *method* instead of handing over the answer — and it draws
real labelled diagrams, science cycles and maths graphs, and can even read a photo
of your written work. It's the difference between "getting the answer" and actually
understanding it.

**Exam Simulator + Predicted Grades.** Students practise under real timed exam
conditions, and the platform predicts their grade from actual performance — with a
confidence band and a trajectory showing whether they're climbing or slipping. No
guesswork, no invented numbers: predictions only appear once there's real evidence
behind them.

**Smart Review (spaced repetition).** The system remembers what each student has
learned and quietly resurfaces anything "at risk of being forgotten" before it's
lost — the proven memory science that turns last-minute cramming into permanent
knowledge.

**Study Planner + Daily Winning Plan.** Builds a personal revision plan in under
five minutes, then each day picks the single best next action so students never sit
staring at "where do I even start?" It even adapts to a daily mood/energy check-in —
lighter days when they're running low.

**Create-your-own revision kit.** AI flashcard maker, quiz generator, revision
notes, mind maps, timelines, essay plans, formula sheets, comparison tables and full
interactive lessons on any topic — a student can turn any subject into a complete
study pack in seconds.

**SkillRush — the Fluency Arena.** A fast, game-style arena (coins, streaks,
leaderboards, class battles) that turns weak skills automatic: times tables, number
bonds, mental arithmetic, spelling, grammar, science facts and more, from Year 1 all
the way up. Includes an AI Coach that explains *why* an answer was wrong.

**Career Passport & GCSE choices.** Matches every student to their best-fit post-16
and career routes — university, degree apprenticeship, T-Level, trade, employment or
self-employment — from their real strengths and interests. For Year 10s it maps their
actual grades to every route's entry requirements and helps them pick the right GCSE
and post-16 subjects, then hands the shortlist to the AI Tutor to talk it through.

**Handwriting & National Checks.** Students photograph handwritten work and get it
marked against a mark scheme *and* on handwriting quality — plus built-in Year 1
Phonics Screening and Year 4 Times Tables Check practice that mirror the real national
assessments.

**Achievements & Motivation Engine.** XP levels, streaks, badges and shareable
certificates that unlock from genuine study activity — the momentum loop that keeps
learners coming back without nagging.

## For Parents

**One dashboard, real answers.** Link a child in seconds with a code or QR, then see
how they're *actually* doing: an Academic Stability score, weekly momentum, subject
mastery heatmaps, an AI grade forecast and early-warning risk alerts — across every
child, in one place.

**Parent Action Cards.** Instead of dense charts, parents get a short weekly list of
the most useful things they can actually do to help — turning worry into a plan.

**The full school loop at home.** Homework and grades, behaviour points, school
notices, timetable, shared documents and a private message thread with the school —
parents finally stop finding things out too late.

**AI Family Support.** A free, confidential whole-family coach that points parents to
trusted UK services for wellbeing, money, employment, apprenticeships and more —
support that goes beyond just the child's grades.

**Privacy the child controls.** Everything a parent sees is consent-gated — the child
can pause sharing at any time — so it builds trust instead of surveillance.

## For Teachers

**Tomorrow's Teaching Brief, written for you.** Every morning the platform reads the
class's live data and hands the teacher ready/at-risk/quiet groupings and the day's
misconception to focus on — with a suggested action for each. Planning that used to
take an evening now takes a glance.

**AI Teaching Assistant.** Generates lesson plans, differentiated tasks, quizzes,
homework, marking frames, parent emails and behaviour strategies in seconds —
automatically pitched to the exact year groups and subjects the teacher is assigned.

**Attendance, assignments & interventions in one command centre.** Take the register,
set and AI-mark homework, spot at-risk students automatically, and generate a
ready-made recovery plan with the student's weak area already filled in — then share
it straight to leadership.

**Fluency Heatmap & live classroom.** See every pupil's fluency skill-by-skill at a
glance, print targeted practice sheets, and launch a browser-based live video lesson
with a pre-lesson briefing on who's behind — no downloads, no extra prep.

**Exam Paper Builder.** Produce a school-branded exam paper of up to 50 questions
*with a full mark scheme*, exportable to Word or PDF, matched to the right stage and
command words.

## For Schools & Multi-Academy Trusts

**AI Academic Operations Centre.** An executive dashboard that turns the whole
school's live data into an academic health index, teaching-quality and progress
indices, exam/university readiness, department and teacher rankings, and a prioritised
list of AI recommendations — the picture a head normally waits weeks for, refreshed
continuously.

**AI School Improvement Engine + Ofsted.** Generates a whole-school improvement plan
across weak departments, attendance, behaviour and safeguarding — and can ingest an
Ofsted report and turn its recommendations into a tracked action plan.

**Equity Engine & global benchmarking.** Monitors progress gaps for SEND, Pupil
Premium and EAL groups, and benchmarks the school against local, national and
international peers — so leaders can prove and close gaps with evidence.

**A real School MIS underneath.** Year groups, admissions pipeline, examinations,
roles & permissions, timetabling, behaviour and rewards, parent communications and
integrations (Google Classroom, Teams, Wonde/SIMS/Arbor) — the operational backbone,
not just analytics.

**Per-child private records, properly permissioned.** Every pupil has a private,
machine-learned learning record with strict, audit-logged access — leadership always,
teachers only for their cohorts, parents only when granted. Compliance built in, not
bolted on.

## For Tutors

**Run your whole tutoring business in one place.** Live earnings, ratings from real
reviews, a booking feed you accept or decline in a tap, a drag-and-drop student
pipeline (enquiry → active → premium), a scheduling calendar and an 8-week revenue
forecast — the admin side of tutoring, handled.

**Pre-Session Intelligence Brief.** For every linked student, the platform auto-writes
what they've done since the last lesson, their weakest topic, and a recommended
objective — so tutors walk into each session fully prepped with zero manual work.

**AI Assistant + branded resources.** Lesson plans, quizzes, homework, exam questions
and a 50-question Exam Paper Builder — all branded to the tutor's own practice and
exportable to Word/PDF.

**A marketplace that sends you students.** Verified, DBS-badged tutor profiles that
parents and students search by subject, price and specialism — a built-in demand
channel, not just software.

**Live browser classroom.** HD video and screen-share lessons with a one-click
"session complete" that logs the revenue straight to earnings.

## Why It's Trusted (the differentiators to lead with)

**End-to-end encrypted.** Each account's learning data is sealed with keys only the
user holds — the servers never see it in plain text. A genuine privacy promise, not
marketing.

**Works offline, installs like an app.** A full offline mode with downloadable study
packs and low-data settings — built for real homes and real connections, including
international reach.

**Evidence-based, never invented.** Grades, weak subjects and predictions only appear
once a student has done real work — so families trust what they see.

**One child, one thread — everyone aligned.** Student, parent, teacher, school and
tutor all see the same consent-controlled picture of the same learner. That connected
loop is the thing no single-purpose app can copy.

**Fair, transparent pricing.** A genuinely free tier to get started (100 credits),
with paid plans metered in clear AI credits — no bill shocks, and payment always
handled by a verified adult.
