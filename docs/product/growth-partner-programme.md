# StudYear Growth Partner Programme

> **Referral + Influencer System — Margin-Protected, Anti-Fraud, Competitive.**
>
> **Core positioning:** *"Earn by helping families, tutors and schools access smarter
> learning — but StudYear only rewards real paid usage, not fake sign-ups."*
>
> **This programme must never pay for noise.** It pays only for **verified, retained,
> revenue-generating users.**
>
> *(Operative growth spec — refines the referral/influencer outline in
> [`commercial-model.md §7–8`](commercial-model.md), which stays on record per the
> [mandate](../REQUIREMENTS-MANDATE.md).)*

## 1. Two programmes, one control system

### A. Standard Referral Programme

For normal users: **students, parents, tutors, school contacts.**

**Reward:** for every successful paid referral, the **referrer receives 250 free ACUs** —
triggered only when the referred user spends at least **£10 minimum paid spend.**

Applies to referrals into any account type:
- Student account
- Parent account
- Private tutor account
- School account

**Rules (no-noise guarantee):**
- **No reward for free sign-ups.**
- **No reward for trial users.**
- **No reward for refunded payments.**
- Reward unlocks **only after the referred user's payment clears and remains valid for 14
  days.**

## 2. Referral upgrade level

Once a referrer successfully refers **20 paid users**, they become a **StudYear Growth
Referrer** and can earn **0.25% of net eligible revenue.**

From that point, **the Influencer Programme rules apply**, meaning:

- monthly cap applies
- customer lifetime cap applies
- anti-fraud checks apply
- clawback applies
- **no self-referral**
- **no duplicate household abuse**
- **no fake school/tutor accounts**
- **no paid incentive fraud**

## 3. Influencer Programme

For **approved** creators, community leaders, tutors, education advocates, school connectors
and **local ambassadors.**

**Commission: 1% lifetime commission**, paid on **net eligible revenue** generated by their
referred customers.

**Net Eligible Revenue** = revenue after deducting:

- refunds
- chargebacks
- VAT/tax where applicable
- Stripe/payment fees
- promo discounts
- fraudulent transactions
- internal credits / free ACUs
- manual admin adjustments
- any unpaid invoice

> **StudYear should never pay commission on gross revenue.**

*(Evolution note: this 1%-lifetime-on-net model supersedes-as-operative the earlier
"20% of first payment only" rule in `commercial-model.md §8`; both stay on record per the
mandate.)*

## 4. Hard payout caps

**Monthly cap:** an influencer can earn a maximum of **£10,000 per month** across all
referred customers.

**Customer cap:** once an influencer earns **£20,000 from one customer**, commission from
that specific customer **ends permanently.** The customer remains a StudYear customer, but
no further commission is paid for that customer.

> **This protects StudYear from long-term margin leakage.**

## 5. Customer types covered

### Student accounts

| Track | Reward |
|---|---|
| Referral reward | **250 ACUs after £10 spend** |
| Influencer commission | **1% of net eligible student revenue** |

**Protected against:** fake student accounts · duplicate child accounts · same-payment-card
abuse · same-device abuse · family self-referral abuse.

### Parent accounts

| Track | Reward |
|---|---|
| Referral reward | **250 ACUs after £10 spend** |
| Influencer commission | **1% of net eligible parent revenue** |

**Protected against:** multiple parent accounts for the same child · duplicate household
gaming · referral swapping between parents · refund abuse.

### Private tutor accounts

| Track | Reward |
|---|---|
| Referral reward | **250 ACUs after £10 spend** |
| Influencer commission | **1% of net eligible tutor revenue** |

**Protected against:** fake tutor registrations · tutors creating fake student accounts ·
tutors referring themselves through student profiles · **commission laundering through
controlled accounts.**
