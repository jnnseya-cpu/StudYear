How it works

Sit with a grown-up. You will see 40 words, one at a time, in two sections of 20. Read each word out loud, sounding out the letters. Some words are alien words 👾 — they are not real! Just read them to the alien.

Your grown-up taps ✓ Read it if you said it correctly, or ✗ Not yet if not. There is no timer — take your time.
