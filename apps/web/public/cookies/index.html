Cookie Policy.

The honest summary: we use the minimum, and none of it is advertising.

Last updated: 2 July 2026

What we store

  • Essential cookies — keeping you signed in, remembering your console, protecting against cross-site attacks. The Service cannot work without these.
  • Local storage — your device keeps working data locally (e.g. draft resources, planner state, streaks) so the tools feel instant. This data stays on your device until you clear it or sync it to your account.
  • Analytics — privacy-respecting, aggregated usage measurement to understand what helps students most. No cross-site tracking.

What we never use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No selling of browsing data.
  • No third-party social-media trackers.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and local storage any time in your browser settings (note: this signs you out and removes locally saved drafts). Because we don't run non-essential trackers, there is no consent banner nagging you on every visit — if that ever changes, we will ask first.

Questions: privacy@studyear.com.
