AI-generated content can be wrong

Tutor explanations, generated notes, quizzes and marked feedback are produced by AI. They are usually accurate — and always fallible. Verify important material against your official exam-board specification and your teacher's guidance. If something looks wrong, report it in-app; content flagged by users is reviewed.

Predicted grades are estimates

Grade trajectories and predictions are statistical estimates built from your activity and mastery data. They exist to guide effort, not to promise outcomes. Real exams involve factors no model can see. No grade, offer or result is ever guaranteed.

Not professional advice

Nothing on StudYear constitutes legal, medical, psychological or formal careers advice. Wellbeing signals (e.g. the burnout indicator) are supportive prompts, not diagnoses — concerns about a child's wellbeing belong with parents, schools and qualified professionals.

Academic integrity

StudYear teaches methods and gives feedback on your work. Submitting AI-generated work as your own may breach your school's or exam board's rules — knowing and following those rules is your responsibility.
