Study science

Study smarter, not harder: the three habits the data keeps confirming

1 · Retrieval beats re-reading. Testing yourself on a topic (flashcards, quizzes, blurting) builds far more durable memory than reading notes again. That's why every StudYear plan is built around active recall sessions, not "read chapter 4".

2 · Spacing beats cramming. Six 40-minute sessions across two weeks outperform one four-hour binge. The planner spaces your weak topics automatically and brings them back just before you'd forget them.

3 · Past papers are the final boss — early. Students who meet real exam questions weeks before the exam consistently outperform those who save them for the end. Your plan switches to timed past-paper mode for the final week by default.
