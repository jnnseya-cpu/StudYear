The closed loop

Assess. Plan. Learn. Improve.

StudYear isn't a pile of study apps — it's one continuous loop inside an AI Academic Operating System. Every stage feeds the next, and the loop never stops running for you, your teachers or your parents.

01 · ASSESS

Find the truth

A free diagnostic looks at your subjects, board, targets and confidence, then pinpoints strong, shaky and missing topics — before they become grade shocks.

02 · PLAN

Strategise

A week-by-week roadmap in under five minutes. High-impact weak topics get more sessions; the final week switches to timed past papers. Miss a session? It rebuilds overnight.

03 · LEARN

Execute

Every tool in one place — 24/7 AI tutor, interactive lessons, resource maker, past papers and instant assignment feedback.

04 · IMPROVE

Measure

Every quiz, session and paper updates your live topic mastery. Your predicted grade refines and your dashboard shows the trajectory. Weak spots loop back to stage 1.

The wallet that can't surprise you

AI actions run on prepaid ACU credits. Your balance is always visible, every action shows its price before you run it, and when the balance reaches zero it simply stops — no surprise bills, ever. The free tier includes enough credits to try the whole loop.

Private by design — end-to-end encrypted

Every learner's personal data is sealed on their own device with keys only they hold — our servers can never read it. School records live behind explicit, logged permissions: the school, the cohort teacher, and a parent only when the school grants it. And the whole OS installs as an app on any phone or tablet, with its own mobile presentation.

One loop, five points of view

Students improve, parents understand, teachers intervene earlier, tutors run their practice, and schools manage performance — all reading the same live mastery record. One truth, five lenses.
