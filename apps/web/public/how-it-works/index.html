The closed loop

Assess → Plan → Learn → Improve.

StudYear is not a pile of study apps — it is one continuous loop. Every stage feeds the next, and the loop never stops running for you.

1 · Assess — find the truth first

Everything starts with a free academic diagnostic. It looks at your subjects, exam board, target grades and confidence, then pinpoints exactly which topics are strong, shaky or missing — before they become grade shocks. Parents and teachers see the same picture you do.

2 · Plan — a strategy, not a timetable

The planner turns your diagnostic into a week-by-week roadmap in under five minutes. Weak, high-grade-impact topics get more sessions; the final week before an exam switches automatically to timed past-paper practice. Miss a session? The plan quietly rebuilds itself overnight.

3 · Learn — every tool, one place

  • AI tutor, 24/7 — step-by-step method help that never just hands you the answer.
  • Resource maker — flashcards, quizzes, notes and mind maps, generated from your syllabus or your own notes.
  • Past-paper locator — papers, mark schemes and examiner reports by board, subject and year.
  • Assignment review — essays marked against the real mark scheme with examiner-style feedback.

4 · Improve — measured, not guessed

Every quiz, session and paper updates your live topic mastery. Your predicted grade refines continuously, and your dashboard shows the trajectory — so improvement is something you can watch, not something you hope for. Weak spots loop straight back into stage 1.

The wallet that can't surprise you

AI actions run on prepaid ACU credits (100 ACUs = £1). Your balance is always visible, every action shows its price before you run it, and when the balance reaches zero it simply stops — no surprise bills, ever. The free tier includes enough credits to try the whole loop.
