Our mission

Every student deserves a strategist.

Private tutors, personal study plans and early intervention used to belong to the families who could afford them. StudYear exists to make that whole apparatus available to every student, for less than the price of a single tuition hour.

What StudYear is

StudYear is an educational operating system — one platform that unifies the five people who make a student succeed:

  • Students get a personal examiner, tutor and strategist that works every day.
  • Parents get clarity and early warnings — momentum and risk, not report-card surprises.
  • Teachers get a class cockpit that shows who needs help weeks before mocks.
  • Schools & trusts get cohort intelligence and inspection-ready evidence of progress.
  • Tutors get a vetted marketplace and arrive at every session already knowing what the student got wrong last week.

All five read the same live mastery record — one truth, five lenses.

What we believe

  • Same lessons, different learners. The teacher teaches once; the OS consolidates a thousand different ways. Personalisation belongs around the lesson, not instead of it.
  • Measured beats guessed. Progress should be visible daily — mastery, adherence and predicted grades, not vibes.
  • Honest pricing. Prepaid credits, a visible balance, a hard stop at zero. No surprise bills, ever.
  • Safeguarding first. Age-appropriate by design, DBS-vetted tutor marketplace, human review available on any AI assessment, and AI providers never train on your data.

Where we work

StudYear is built for the UK curriculum first — Primary, KS3, GCSE, A-Level, IB, BTEC, 11+/SATs and university study skills, across AQA, Edexcel, OCR and WJEC — operated from England & Wales under UK GDPR.
