Our mission

Every student deserves a strategist.

Private tutors, personal study plans and early intervention used to belong to the families who could afford them. StudYear exists to make that whole apparatus — now supercharged by AI — available to every student, for less than the price of a single tuition hour.

The OS today

The current release already ships the full loop end to end: end-to-end encrypted student data, a private permissioned school record and machine-learning study model per child, a school Data Intake Hub so every metric traces to its input, a My School area students open from home, Socratic tutor modes, the Exam Paper Builder, SkillRush™ and Maths Heroes fluency games, parent Action Cards, teacher and tutor daily briefs, Local Authority impact intelligence — and the whole OS installs as an app on any phone or tablet.

What we believe

  • Same lessons, different learners. The teacher teaches once; the OS consolidates a thousand different ways. Personalisation belongs around the lesson, not instead of it.
  • Measured beats guessed. Progress should be visible daily — mastery, adherence and predicted grades, not vibes.
  • Honest pricing. Prepaid credits, a visible balance, a hard stop at zero. No surprise bills, ever.
  • Safeguarding first. Age-appropriate by design, DBS-vetted tutor marketplace, human review on any AI assessment, end-to-end encryption, and AI providers never train on your data.
