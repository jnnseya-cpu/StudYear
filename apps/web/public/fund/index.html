🌍 Diaspora & sponsor funding

Fund a student's learning — from anywhere.

A parent working abroad. An auntie in another city. A church, a mosque, a community fund, a company sponsoring local kids. Anyone, anywhere in the world, can power a student's StudYear learning — without sharing passwords, bank details or accounts.

How it works — three steps

01 · CHOOSE

Pick a pack

From £3 to £30 — each pack is a bundle of ACU learning credits with a clear study estimate (≈ 30 ACUs per focused hour of AI-powered study).

02 · SEND

Get a gift code

You receive a one-time gift code — send it by WhatsApp, SMS or any way you like. You can lock it to the student's email so only they can use it.

03 · LEARN

They redeem it

The student pastes the code in Plans & top-up and the credits land in their wallet instantly — with your name in their "My supporters" list.

Safe by design

Gift codes give a supporter no access whatsoever to the student's account, messages or data — the student's personal data stays end-to-end encrypted with keys only they hold. A code is worth its credits and nothing more; if it's locked to an email, only that student can redeem it. Credits never expire into a subscription: no auto-renewal, no surprise bills, ever.

International card and mobile-money payments (including BitriPay) settle at checkout as billing partners go live; gift codes work today.
